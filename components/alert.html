Set time to live for Alert

This alert will dismiss after 5 seconds.

 

<elemnt-alert ttl="5000">
    <span slot="title">Auto Dismiss Alert</span>
    <p slot="content">This alert will dismiss after 5 seconds.</p>
</elemnt-alert>

Alert Positions

You can position top-left, top, top-right, bottom-left, bottom, and bottom-right.

You can also pass a `launch-event` which is an event name which the alert will listen for to become visible.

Top Right Alert

This alert is positioned at the top right.

 Launch top right alert 

<elemnt-alert launch-event="launchTopRightAlert" position="top-right" ttl="5000" variant="success">
    <span slot="title">Top Right Alert</span>
    <p slot="content">This alert is positioned at the top right.</p>
</elemnt-alert>
Top Left Alert

This alert is positioned at the top left.

 Launch top left alert 

<elemnt-alert launch-event="launchTopLeftAlert" position="top-left" ttl="5000" variant="error">
    <span slot="title">Top Left Alert</span> ;
