Tabs

Tabs are a great way to organize content into separate views. You can create a tabbed interface using the elemnt-tabs component.

Tab 1 Title Tab 2 Title Tab 3 Title

This is the content for Tab 1.

This is the content for Tab 2.

This is the content for Tab 3.

 

<elemnt-tabs>
    <elemnt-tab name="Tab 1">
        Tab 1 Title
    </elemnt-tab>
    <elemnt-tab name="Tab 2">     
        Tab 2 Title
    </elemnt-tab>
    <elemnt-tab name="Tab 3">
        Tab 3 Title
    </elemnt-tab>

    <elemnt-tab-content name="Tab 1">
        <p>This is the content for Tab 1.</p>
    </elemnt-tab-content>
    <elemnt-tab-content name="Tab 2">
        <p>This is the content for Tab 2.</p>
    </elemnt-tab-content>
    <elemnt-tab-content name="Tab 3">
        <p>This is the content for Tab 3.</p>
    </elemnt-tab-content>
</elemnt-tabs>
